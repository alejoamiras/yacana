## Facts the plan rests on

- `claim` (`protocol/contracts/yacana_miner/src/main.nr:173-225`) verifies the proof at `:218`, then
  `ticket_digest(proof)` `:219`, the target `:220` and the nullifier `:221`. `ticket.nr:6-13` hashes all 410 raw fields.
- The fixture proof's 36 points: P0, P1 and the three lookup commitments are all zero (infinity is `(0, 0)` in
  `BB/stdlib/primitives/field/field_conversion.hpp:114`). Every other point is canonical, with the largest `hi` limb
  at `0x30184f32…` < `Q_HI`. The 24 Gemini folds are non-constant, including the padding rounds.
- TXE fabricated proofs are all zero except `proof[0] = k` (small), so they stay canonical.
- **Measured baseline** (committed artifact, `bb gates --scheme chonk`): `claim` 31,985 gates, VK `log n = 15`;
  `claim_from_l1` 34,823, `exit_to_l1` 19,500, `send_ahead` 19,499, all at `log n = 16`. The audit's 30,207 dates
  from before the bridge.
- **Measured spike** (the three-check form over the 34 non-io points, compiled with `aztec compile`): `claim`
  33,399 gates (+1,414, +272 ACIR opcodes), VK `log n = 16`. The padded size is the next power of two of trace plus
  lookup tables (`BB/ultra_honk/prover_instance.cpp:129-144`), so no form of the check fits in 783 gates of headroom.
- Chonk sizes each circuit on its own (`BB/chonk/mock_circuit_producer.hpp:231-270`: kernels at 2^17–2^18,
  trailing kernels ≤ 2^17).
- `wallet.registerContract` performs no artifact/class validation. A mismatched artifact surfaces only when a call
  is simulated (`node_modules/@aztec/wallet-sdk/dest/base-wallet/base_wallet.js:255-262`,
  `node_modules/@aztec/pxe/dest/pxe.js:566-569`). The site serves the committed artifact
  (`protocol/contracts/artifacts/`), which CI re-derives from source.
- The live deployment is `deployments/testnet.json` (`minerClassId 0x0950…d63a`). The miner has no upgrade path
  (`main.nr:3`: "no privileged call remains once bind_token has run").
- `aztec` is not on `PATH` in `bun run`'s shell on this machine. Prefix with
  `PATH=$HOME/.aztec/versions/5.2.0/bin:$HOME/.aztec/versions/5.2.0/node_modules/.bin:$PATH`.

## Collision risks

- "slots" already names the storage-slot table (`packages/miner-core/src/slots.ts`, `gen-slots.ts`). The new
  generated file is `proof_points.nr`.
- "canonical" already names Presto's whole-field check. Comments on the new check say "one encoding per point" /
  "coordinate < q".
- `vectors.nr` is generated. Hand-written tests go in a new `test/` module, not in the template.
