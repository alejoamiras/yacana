why proving

The race to mine is a race to make Aztec fast.

Mining runs Barretenberg, the prover behind every Aztec transaction. Miners earn more by proving faster, and every speedup that lands in Barretenberg makes Aztec faster and cheaper for everyone.

1 · earnMiners want more winsMore proofs a minute, more draws.
2 · optimizeSo they prove fasterNative provers like Presto, leaner WASM, better hardware.
3 · upstreamBarretenberg gets fasterSpeedups that land upstream reach every Aztec prover.
4 · growAztec gets cheaper to useQuicker private transactions draw more people, and more miners.

The difficulty keeps issuance at 4 wins every 5 min, however fast proving gets.
