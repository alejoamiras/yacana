===== Fly-A =====
how it works
Prove. Hash. Claim.
why proving
The race to mine is a race to make Aztec fast.
Mining runs Barretenberg, the prover behind every Aztec transaction. Miners earn more by proving faster, and every speedup that lands in Barretenberg makes Aztec faster and cheaper for everyone.
1 · earn Miners want more wins More proofs a minute, more draws.
2 · optimize So they prove faster Native provers like Presto, leaner WASM, better hardware.
3 · upstream Barretenberg gets faster Speedups that land upstream reach every Aztec prover.
4 · grow Aztec gets cheaper to use Quicker private transactions draw more people, and more miners.
The difficulty keeps issuance at 4 wins every 5 min, however fast proving gets.
